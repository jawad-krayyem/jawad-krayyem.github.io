StudyFlow privacy policy

Updated 31 October 2026

About StudyFlow

StudyFlow is an offline Android study organizer and puzzle arcade developed by Jawad Krayyem. This policy applies to the offline StudyFlow 2.4 release and later releases that use the same data practices.

Study content and game progress

Courses, classes, homework, exams, focus history, imported files and Arcade progress are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. StudyFlow does not transmit this content to the developer or to a server. Older study records retained during upgrades remain local and are included in your backups. There is no StudyFlow account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics service, AI service, subscription or in-app purchase in this release.

Files and backups you choose

Android’s file picker provides access to files you select. Imported copies are kept for offline use. You can export a backup containing your records and original imported files to a location you choose. Backups are not encrypted by StudyFlow; protect them as you would your original documents. Importing a backup replaces the current workspace after confirmation. Export a backup first to preserve the current workspace. If you choose another app to view or share a document, that app receives the selected copy and handles it under its own privacy practices. A storage provider you choose, including a cloud drive, controls copies stored with that provider.

Notification permission

StudyFlow requests Android notification permission for local reminders. Class reminders are scheduled 15 minutes before a class and at its start; focus timers and exam reminders also use local notifications. You can deny or revoke notification permission in Android Settings and continue using the other features. Delivery timing depends on Android notification and battery settings. The app does not request broad storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Retention and deletion

Your content is retained on the device until you remove it through available app controls or clear the app’s storage or uninstall StudyFlow. Save any content you need before clearing storage or uninstalling. Android automatic app backup is disabled for this release. Clearing local data does not erase exported backups, original files held by other apps or storage providers, or copies you shared. Delete those copies through the destination’s controls. The developer has no server copy to retrieve or delete.

Google Play and support

Google Play handles the paid app download and its payment records under Google’s policies. StudyFlow does not receive your card or bank details. If you email support, the information you choose to send is processed to answer your request and may be retained as part of that correspondence. Do not include private study documents unless you want to share them for support. For questions or requests about support correspondence, contact krayyemjawad@gmail.com.

Audience and changes

StudyFlow is intended primarily for teens and adults. It does not offer social features or public user profiles. Changes to the app’s data practices will be reflected in an updated policy, available in the app and from its Google Play listing.
