FRACTURE Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FRACTURE (“the Game”) handles information when you use the Android application.

Developer / Publisher:
Jawad Krayyem

Privacy contact:
krayyemjawad@gmail.com

1. Information FRACTURE Stores Locally

FRACTURE may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

- game progress
- unlocked or owned weapons
- equipped weapon state
- in-game currency
- run history
- settings and preferences
- control and graphics settings
- local gameplay state needed to operate the Game

This information is generally stored on the device and is used to provide gameplay functionality and preserve player progress.

FRACTURE does not currently require a user account to play.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

FRACTURE may use third-party advertising and consent-management services.

These services may process information such as:

- advertising identifiers
- device identifiers
- device and operating-system information
- IP address
- general location information derived from network data
- ad interactions
- ad impressions
- consent and privacy choices
- SDK diagnostic information
- app and session information

The exact information processed may depend on your region, privacy choices, device settings, and the third-party provider.

FRACTURE currently uses or may use:

- Unity LevelPlay
- Unity Ads
- ironSource / LevelPlay advertising services
- InMobi Choice CMP for privacy and consent management
- Ad Quality components included with the advertising stack

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Purpose of Advertising Data

Advertising-related information may be processed for purposes such as:

- delivering advertisements
- selecting advertisements
- measuring advertising performance
- fraud prevention
- security
- frequency management
- consent and privacy-choice handling
- analytics and service improvement where permitted
- personalized advertising where legally permitted and where valid consent or another lawful basis exists

FRACTURE does not intentionally enable personalized advertising where required consent has not been obtained.

4. Consent Management

FRACTURE uses a Consent Management Platform (CMP) to manage privacy choices in regions where consent or opt-out rights apply.

Depending on your region, you may be shown options to:

- accept or refuse optional advertising processing
- opt out of sale or sharing where applicable
- opt out of targeted advertising where applicable
- review or change your privacy choices

Your consent choices may be stored by the CMP so they can persist between app sessions.

Where required, advertising SDK initialization is blocked until an appropriate privacy decision has been resolved.

5. Children and Teen Users

FRACTURE is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Advertising and privacy handling for teen users may be restricted depending on applicable regional law and platform requirements.

If the Game cannot determine that advertising processing is permitted for a user, advertising may remain disabled.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with or processed by third-party advertising, mediation, fraud-prevention, consent-management, and technical service providers as necessary to provide those services.

FRACTURE does not sell personal information directly to data brokers.

However, certain advertising activities may be considered a “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising” under some privacy laws. Where applicable, users are provided with relevant opt-out choices.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by FRACTURE and its service providers.

Third-party providers are responsible for the security of information processed within their own systems.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is:

- overwritten by game progress
- reset through available game functionality
- cleared through Android app-storage controls
- removed when the Game is uninstalled, subject to Android and backup behavior

Third-party providers may retain advertising, diagnostic, and consent information according to their own retention policies and applicable law.

FRACTURE does not currently maintain a centralized player-account database.

9. Data Deletion

Because FRACTURE currently does not require a player account and primarily stores gameplay data locally, you can generally remove local game data by:

- clearing FRACTURE’s app data through Android settings, or
- uninstalling the Game

For data processed by third-party advertising or consent providers, deletion or privacy requests may need to be directed to the relevant provider according to their privacy policy.

For privacy questions or requests relating specifically to FRACTURE, contact:

krayyemjawad@gmail.com

10. International Processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, providers may use legal safeguards for international transfers.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- access certain personal information
- request correction
- request deletion
- object to or restrict certain processing
- withdraw consent
- opt out of targeted advertising
- opt out of sale or sharing

Where supported, these choices can be accessed through FRACTURE’s privacy settings or the consent-management interface.

12. Third-Party Privacy Policies

For more information about third-party data handling, consult the privacy documentation of:

- Unity / LevelPlay
- Unity Ads
- ironSource
- InMobi Choice CMP
- other advertising providers shown in the consent-management interface

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FRACTURE’s features, advertising providers, privacy practices, or legal requirements change.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be changed when updates are published.
