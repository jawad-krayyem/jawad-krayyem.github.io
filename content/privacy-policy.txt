FRACTURE Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FRACTURE handles information when you use the Android game.

FRACTURE is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.


1. Information FRACTURE may process

FRACTURE may process or store information needed to operate the game, remember settings, manage privacy choices, and determine whether optional advertising may be used.

This may include:

- gameplay and application settings;
- the age range selected by the user;
- privacy and consent choices;
- applicable privacy or regional state;
- advertising-provider permissions;
- technical information needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the game;
- advertising-related information where advertising is permitted by the user's privacy choices and applicable rules.

FRACTURE stores the selected age range rather than a date of birth.


2. Advertising and age

Optional advertising is currently restricted to users treated by FRACTURE's privacy controls as adults aged 18 or older.

Advertising remains disabled for:

- users under 18;
- users whose age range is unresolved;
- users whose privacy or consent state is unresolved;
- users whose applicable privacy state does not permit advertising.

Refusing advertising consent does not prevent normal gameplay, although optional features that depend on advertising, such as an ad-supported reward, may be unavailable.


3. Consent management

FRACTURE uses InMobi Choice as its consent-management platform.

The consent manager may be used to present privacy information and collect or store applicable privacy choices.

Loading or displaying the consent manager does not itself constitute advertising consent.

Advertising is not authorized merely because the consent manager loaded successfully.

Where applicable, FRACTURE uses privacy signals associated with its advertising providers before allowing advertising initialization.


4. Advertising providers

FRACTURE currently integrates advertising technology associated with:

Unity Ads / Unity Technologies

ironSource / Unity LevelPlay

Privacy choices for these providers may be handled through applicable consent frameworks and provider-specific privacy signals.

Each provider may process information according to its own privacy policy, legal obligations, and retention practices.

FRACTURE does not claim that every advertising provider uses the same retention period or stops all previously performed processing when consent is withdrawn.


5. United States privacy handling

Advertising is currently disabled for users whose privacy state is handled under the United States regulatory path.

United States privacy choices may still be available even while advertising is disabled.

Unknown or unresolved regional or privacy states do not permit advertising initialization.

Privacy controls and the consent manager may therefore remain available independently of whether advertisements are currently delivered.


6. Local privacy authorization record

In addition to gameplay settings, FRACTURE stores the selected age range and a local privacy authorization record on the device.

This record is used to determine whether advertising may initialize under the current privacy configuration.

The record may include:

- the applicable privacy or regional state;
- advertising-provider permissions;
- the privacy-policy or notice revision;
- the consent-manager configuration;
- the time associated with the relevant privacy choice;
- technical hashes used to detect whether the underlying consent state or configuration has changed.

This record is not a player account and is not intended to establish or verify a person's identity.


7. Authorization validity

An advertising authorization is treated as valid for no more than 30 days.

It may become invalid sooner if information relevant to the authorization changes, including:

- privacy or consent choices;
- selected age range;
- applicable regional state;
- privacy-policy or notice version;
- consent-manager configuration;
- provider permissions.

The 30-day authorization period is an authorization limit. It does not mean that every locally stored privacy record is automatically deleted after 30 days.


8. Local retention

Local privacy information may remain on the device until it is replaced, reset, revoked, cleared through Android application settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Consent-management and advertising providers may retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.


9. Reviewing or changing privacy choices

You can review or change advertising-provider choices from:

Settings → Privacy → Review privacy choices

Where applicable, United States privacy choices are available through the corresponding privacy controls.

Changes should be saved through the consent manager.


10. Refusal and withdrawal

Refusal, withdrawal, missing permissions, or unresolved privacy state do not become advertising consent merely because the consent manager has loaded.

When FRACTURE's advertising authorization is turned off or becomes invalid, FRACTURE blocks further advertising requests under that authorization.

Changing FRACTURE's local advertising authorization does not necessarily rewrite every preference maintained by the consent-management platform.

To change provider-specific consent preferences, use:

Settings → Privacy → Review privacy choices

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that was lawfully performed before consent was withdrawn.

Withdrawal also does not by itself erase information already processed by third parties or necessarily unload software components that were already initialized.

Requests relating to access, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights are separate from changing an advertising-consent preference.


11. Security, fraud prevention, and technical operation

Information may be processed as necessary to help operate the game, preserve privacy choices, maintain security, prevent or detect fraud, diagnose errors, and protect the integrity of the application.

FRACTURE does not use the privacy authorization record as proof of identity.


12. Your privacy rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include rights to:

- access personal data;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- request deletion where applicable;
- restrict certain processing;
- object to certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
- receive certain data in a portable format where applicable.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of valid consent before withdrawal.

Where the GDPR applies, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

Additional rights may apply depending on your jurisdiction.


13. Third-party privacy policies

FRACTURE relies on third-party services for consent management and advertising functionality.

You should also review the applicable privacy information provided by:

InMobi Choice

Unity Technologies / Unity Ads / LevelPlay

ironSource

Those providers are responsible for their own processing practices where they act independently.


14. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable, those providers are responsible for using appropriate mechanisms required by applicable data-protection law for their processing activities.


15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FRACTURE's privacy practices, providers, consent configuration, advertising behavior, or legal requirements change.

When material changes are made, the updated policy will show a revised "Last updated" date.

A change to the privacy notice or consent configuration may also cause FRACTURE to require privacy authorization to be reviewed again before advertising can initialize.


16. Contact

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns relating to FRACTURE, please use the developer contact information provided on FRACTURE's Google Play Store listing or the official FRACTURE website.
